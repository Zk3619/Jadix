It includes the native D3D11 backend and the corresponding shader translation changes; the original implementation notes, licenses and source identities are retained under `upstream/`. The facade exposes the integrated low level APIs as `wgpu::hal` and `wgpu::naga`. Original copyright and license files are retained: LICENSE.APACHE, LICENSE.MIT.
